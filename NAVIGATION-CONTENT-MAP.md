## Page-specific content summaries

### About

Full-service brokerage operated by David & Simone Karstedt, expert Realtors with over 100-years combined experience, Clear Lake NASA expertise, Texas Monthly Top 3% recognition, local neighborhood knowledge, market analysis, buyer/seller representation, REO/foreclosure, commercial real estate, new construction, renting, property management, and leasing.

### Testimonials

Review collection from dozens of customers. Repeated themes include responsiveness, local knowledge, negotiation, patience, first-time buyer support, buying and selling support, property management, and personalized service.

### Home Buying

Pre-approval, seller confidence, faster loan processing, concessions/addendums, inventory, interest rates, competition, contingencies, foreclosure expertise, buyer services, mortgage guidance, school/community information, relocation, map search, MLS search, alerts, closing support.

### Home Selling

Market analysis, staging, renovations, documentation, repairs, cleaning, decluttering, curb appeal, Rent-Back clauses, sale-before-buy strategy, cash offers, contingencies, MLS/Zillow/Trulia/Realtor/Homes marketing, red-flag review, status updates, negotiation, closing support.

### Homes for Rent

Rental listings, real-time search, leasing, property management, MLS listing, tenant interviews, background checks, leases, deposits, rent market analysis, and neighborhood/commute/school guidance.

### Agents

David Karstedt is Broker/Owner. The team promises local experience, customer service, real-time listings, and a professional caring approach from first conversation through closing and funding. The agent directory contains nine public agent profiles.

### Contact

Monday through Saturday, 9am–6pm. Phone: `281-241-3121`. Email: `info@1sttexasrealtors.com`. Contact CTA points to Meet Our Agents.

### Privacy Policy

Membership registration data is used for custom Home Searches. The page states information is not shared, distributed, sold, or used and names GoDaddy, WordPress, IDX Broker, and Google Recaptcha as security partners. Registered users can unsubscribe through account settings or contact.
